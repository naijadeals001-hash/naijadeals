## PART 1 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Vision
To be the single, high-trust entry point for the African digital economy.

### Mission
To provide a "Verified Path" for economic growth by connecting 1 billion Africans through a unified identity, wallet, and service ecosystem.

### The Problem
African digital commerce is currently fragmented across single-vertical apps (Uber for rides, Jumia for shopping, OPay for payments) that suffer from high customer acquisition costs (CAC), low trust, inconsistent logistics, and siloed data.

### The Solution: NaijaDeals
NaijaDeals is a unified Super App Operating System. By consolidating commerce, logistics, and finance onto a single high-trust foundation (The "Verified Path"), we eliminate siloed friction and create a compounding trust moat that single-vertical competitors cannot match.

### Strategic Positioning
NaijaDeals sits at the intersection of **Amazon's efficiency**, **Uber's mobility**, **Airbnb's infrastructure audit**, and **WeChat's ecosystem lock-in**, localized specifically for the unique challenges of the African continent.

## PART 4 — REVENUE MODEL (10-STEP MONETIZATION)

NaijaDeals captures value at every touchpoint of the transaction lifecycle.

1.  **Marketplace Commissions**: 8%–25% (tiered by vertical: Shop/Eats/Gigs).
2.  **Transaction Fees**: 1.5% + fixed fee for payment processing and FX.
3.  **Escrow Protection**: 1%–3% fee for high-value secure transaction management.
4.  **Subscription Tiers**: NaijaPrime (Consumer) and Pro (Merchant/Driver) monthly fees.
5.  **Advertising**: Sponsored search ranking, hyper-local ad network, and brand takeovers.
6.  **Enterprise SaaS**: Licensing the "Verified Path" infrastructure to governments/corps.
7.  **Logistics Markup**: Spreads on long-haul freight and inter-hub transfers.
8.  **Financial Services**: Interest on credit lending (Airtime/Data) and capital reserves.
9.  **AI Insights**: Selling anonymized market demand data to FMCGs.
10. **API Access**: Usage-based billing for 3rd party developers and integrators.

# NaijaDeals Master Ecosystem User Journey

## Core Philosophy
Transforming the African digital experience from a series of fragmented apps into a single, high-trust lifecycle. Every user action builds data equity, trust scores, and economic value.

## The Definitive Lifecycle
1.  **Open App (Access)**: Biometric authentication, high-speed regional gateway.
2.  **Discover (Explore)**: Universal Search & Aura AI recommendations.
3.  **Compare (Logic)**: Deterministic product scoring & price parity.
4.  **Learn (Growth)**: Merchant Academy, vocational certifications, skills.
5.  **Buy (Commerce)**: Unified checkout, escrow-backed multi-currency payments.
6.  **Track (Logistics)**: Real-time GPS mission monitoring, cold-chain transparency.
7.  **Earn (Economy)**: Driver-first commissions, vendor payouts, gig fulfillment.
8.  **Invest (Finance)**: Capital reserves, crypto liquidity, regional expansion bonds.
9.  **Communicate (Trust)**: Masked calling, in-app chat, dispute resolution.
10. **Stay (Lifestyle)**: Shortlet booking, property management, local residency.

## 2. Missing Features & Workflows Report

### 🚩 Critical Business Process Gaps
- **NaijaShop**: Missing a **Merchant Dispute Response Workflow** (User currently has a resolution deep-dive, but the merchant response UI is fragmented).
- **NaijaEats**: Missing **Group Ordering & Bill Splitting**—a high-viral-loop feature for African social dining.
- **NaijaDrive**: Missing **Driver Asset Financing Integration** (Direct links from Driver App to NaijaAuto for vehicle upgrades).
- **NaijaGigs**: Missing **Contract Versioning & Redlining** (Collaboration vault exists, but formal legal negotiation UI is light).
- **Ecosystem**: Missing a **Unified Returns & Reverse Logistics Pipeline** (The "Last Mile" of commerce is designed, but the "First Mile" of returns is not).

## 4. Missing Mobile Experiences

### 📱 Gaps in the Mobile Ecosystem
- **Vendor Mobile**: Comprehensive **Inventory Management on Mobile** is currently missing (Hub exists, but granular stock control is needed).
- **Host Mobile**: **Property Inspection Checklist** (A tool for cleaners/managers to verify standards before guest arrival).
- **Creator Mobile**: **Live Performance Interaction Hub** (A mobile view for artists to see live tips/chats while streaming from a desktop).
- **Executive Mobile**: **Ecosystem Kill-Switch (Mobile Version)** for Super-Admins to halt transactions in case of regional crisis.

## 5. Missing AI & Monetization Opportunities

### 🤖 AI Opportunities
- **AI-Driven Local Pricing**: Automatically adjusting NaijaShop prices based on "Local Market Days" and regional scarcity.
- **Predictive Inventory Handoff**: AI predicting when a NaijaEats kitchen will run low and auto-triggering a NaijaWholesale restock order via NaijaSend.
- **Aura AI Legal Assistant**: Analyzing Gigs/Contractor agreements to flag regional compliance risks in real-time.

### 💰 Monetization Gaps
- **Ecosystem Data Marketplace**: Selling anonymized market demand insights to FMCGs (Fast-Moving Consumer Goods companies).
- **Verification-as-a-Service**: Offering the NaijaDeals KYC/Trust score as a plug-in for external African websites.
- **Ads in Logistics**: Monetizing the "Track Package" screen with hyper-local promotions for upcoming stops.

## 6. Future Expansion Roadmap

### 🟢 Must Have (Pre-Launch)
1. **Reverse Logistics (Returns) Hub**: Essential for e-commerce trust.
2. **Unified Terms & Privacy Acceptance**: Single legal gate for the Super App.
3. **Emergency Regional Handoff (Mobile)**: Safety protocol for drivers/couriers.

### 🟡 Should Have (Q1 Post-Launch)
1. **NaijaHealth Provider Portal**: Essential to activate the healthcare vertical.
2. **B2B Bulk Negotiation AI**: To scale NaijaWholesale.
3. **Developer API Key Management**: To grow the 3rd party ecosystem.

### 🔵 Nice To Have
1. **VR Real Estate Tours**: For high-end NaijaStay properties.
2. **AI Voice Translation**: Real-time translation for masked calls between Francophone and Anglophone users.

Proceed to design the **NaijaHealth Provider Portal** to close the last major vertical gap.

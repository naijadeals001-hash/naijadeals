# NaijaDeals Development Philosophy: Mobile First

## Core Principle
The majority of African users access the internet through mobile devices. Therefore, NaijaDeals is engineered, designed, and optimized for mobile-first delivery.

## Target Device Standards

### 1. Mobile (Android & iPhone)
- **Priority**: Primary interaction point for 99% of users.
- **Standards**: Fast loading (<2s on 3G/4G), low data consumption, high-contrast UI for outdoor visibility, and large tap targets (min 44x44px).
- **Features**: Native-like responsiveness, biometric security, and offline-first capabilities.

### 2. Tablets
- **Priority**: Growing segment for business and entertainment.
- **Standards**: Adaptive layouts that utilize increased screen real estate without losing mobile density. Support for multi-window multitasking.

### 3. Desktop
- **Priority**: Administrative, merchant management, and high-stakes financial operations.
- **Standards**: High-density command centers, complex data visualization, and keyboard-optimized workflows.

## Global Consistency
Every service (Shop, Eats, Drive, etc.) must maintain functional parity across all device classes while adapting its UI to the specific constraints and opportunities of the hardware.

## Brand & Style
The design system is built upon the philosophy of **Cognitive Clarity**. It serves as the intelligent layer of the NaijaDeals ecosystem, transforming complex market data into actionable insights. The visual language is "Intelligence-First," prioritizing precision, speed, and high-level trust.

The style is **Modern Corporate with a High-Tech Edge**. It blends the grounded stability of traditional enterprise software with the ethereal, glowing aesthetics of advanced AI. By using sharp execution and purposeful whitespace, the system achieves an "Amazon-tier" standard of functional sophistication. The emotional response is one of empowered confidence—users should feel they are using a tool that is faster and smarter than the competition.

## Colors
The palette is anchored by **Emerald Velocity (#064E3B)**, a deep, authoritative green that signals growth and stability. This is the foundation for all primary navigation and structural elements.

To represent the "Aura" AI influence, a secondary accent of **Soft Violet (#8B5CF6)** is used for active AI states, suggestions, and generative moments. **Glowing Teal (#2DD4BF)** serves as a tertiary "success" or "optimization" color, specifically for data highlights and positive intelligence trends. 

Neutral tones are cool-skewed to maintain a clinical, professional atmosphere. While the default mode is light for maximum readability during intense data analysis, a "High-Trust Dark" variant is utilized for immersive dashboard environments.

## Typography
Typography is engineered for **data density**. **Manrope** is used for headlines to provide a modern, balanced, and approachable character. For the core UI and body text, **Inter** is the workhorse, selected for its exceptional legibility at small sizes and its systematic, neutral tone.

To emphasize the "Intelligence-First" nature of this design system, **JetBrains Mono** is introduced for technical labels, metadata, and data points. This monospaced font signals precision and algorithmic accuracy. 

Line heights are kept tight but comfortable to allow more information to be visible above the fold. Large display sizes are reserved for high-level summaries and key "Aura" metrics.

## Layout & Spacing
The design system utilizes a **12-column fixed grid** for desktop environments to maintain rigid control over complex data visualization. On mobile, the system transitions to a fluid, single-column stack with 16px safe margins.

A strict **8px spacing scale** ensures a rhythmic, predictable layout. Components are categorized into three density levels:
1. **Compact:** For data-heavy tables and sidebars (4px-8px gaps).
2. **Standard:** For general UI and input forms (12px-16px gaps).
3. **Editorial:** For the "Intelligence Briefing" modules and marketing landing pages (24px+ gaps).

Layouts should favor vertical stacks with clear horizontal dividers to mimic the efficient scanning patterns of Amazon-tier interfaces.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is used sparingly to signify importance and hierarchy. This design system employs **Tonal Layers** as its primary elevation strategy. 

Backgrounds use a "Base" neutral, while "Surface" containers sit on top with 1px borders in a slightly lighter or darker neutral tint. 

For AI-specific elements, the system uses **Glassmorphism and Glows**. An "Aura Insight" card will feature a subtle backdrop blur (12px) and a very thin, 1px semi-transparent border colored with the secondary Violet or Teal. This "glow" effect creates a visual distinction between standard platform data and AI-generated intelligence, making the AI elements appear to float slightly above the core UI.

## Shapes
The shape language is **Soft (Level 1)**. Elements like buttons and input fields use a 4px (0.25rem) corner radius. This choice reflects a professional, precise, and serious aesthetic.

Larger containers, such as "Intelligence Briefing" modules, may use `rounded-lg` (8px) to feel more contained and modular. Circular shapes are strictly reserved for user avatars or specific "AI Pulse" indicators to represent the system's "always-on" monitoring capability. Avoid "Pill" shapes for primary buttons to maintain the sophisticated, geometric rigor of the brand.

## Components

### AI Action Buttons
Primary buttons are high-contrast, using the Emerald Green. However, "AI Action" buttons use a gradient shift from Primary Green to the Soft Violet accent. They should include a small "spark" or "Aura" icon (SVG) to signify that clicking the button triggers an automated or intelligent process.

### Aura Insight Cards
These are the signature components. They feature a white or very light gray surface with a left-side accent border (4px) in Glowing Teal or Soft Violet. They include a "Confidence Score" badge in the top right corner using the `data-mono` typography style.

### Intelligence Briefing Modules
Large-format containers designed for deep-dive reading. They use a slightly larger `body-lg` font for the lead paragraph and include "Key Takeaway" chips at the top. These modules should be separated by clear, low-contrast horizontal rules.

### Input Fields & Controls
Fields use a 1px border (#E2E8F0) that shifts to the Soft Violet accent on focus. Checkboxes and radios use the Emerald Green for the checked state to maintain the ecosystem's core branding.

### Aura Pulse (Indicator)
A small, glowing animated dot component used next to real-time data feeds. It uses the Tertiary Teal with a soft pulse animation (opacity shift) to indicate live intelligence gathering.

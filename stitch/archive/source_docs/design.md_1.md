NaijaDeals.com Design System
Design NaijaDeals.com as a mobile-first African super app ecosystem combining commerce, food delivery, gigs, shortlets, logistics, rides, streaming, and wallet services.
Design Goal
Create a clean, premium, fast, and trustworthy interface inspired by the best mobile patterns from Amazon, Airbnb, Uber, Uber Eats, Fiverr, SoundCloud, TikTok, and Temu — but do not copy them.
NaijaDeals must feel original, African, modern, and globally competitive.

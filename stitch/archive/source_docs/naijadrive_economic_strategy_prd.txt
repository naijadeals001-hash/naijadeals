# NaijaDeals Driver-First Economics PRD

## Core Philosophy
NaijaDeals shall maintain a driver-first earnings model. The platform should seek profitability through ecosystem volume, subscriptions, advertising, merchant services, and premium offerings rather than excessive driver commissions.

## Commission Structure (Dynamic)
Base platform fees are calculated based on ride tier and real-time algorithmic adjustments (6% - 15% range).

| Ride Tier | Platform Fee Range |
| :--- | :--- |
| Economy & Standard | 8% – 12% |
| Comfort & SUV | 10% – 12% |
| Executive | 12% – 15% |
| Luxury & Super Luxury | 15% – 18% |

## Driver Subscription Option (The "Pro" Model)
Drivers can opt-out of standard commissions by paying a monthly access fee and a minimal transaction fee.

| Tier | Monthly Subscription | Transaction Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Economy Driver | ₦10,000 | 3% – 5% |
| Standard Driver | ₦15,000 | 3% – 5% |
| Executive Driver | ₦25,000 | 3% – 5% |

## Competitive Advantages
1. **Lower Commissions:** Significantly lower than the 20-25% industry standard.
2. **Ecosystem Multitasking:** Drivers can earn from Rides, Eats, Fresh, Send, and Transit.
3. **AI Dispatching:** Maximizing driver utilization across all verticals.
4. **Subscription Predictability:** Fixed costs for high-volume drivers.

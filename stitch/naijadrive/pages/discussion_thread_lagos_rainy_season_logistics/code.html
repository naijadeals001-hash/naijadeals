Best practices for Lagos delivery during rainy season

With the heavy downpours recently, especially in areas like Lekki, Victoria Island, and parts of the Mainland, standard delivery times are becoming impossible to hit. Many of our customers are complaining about delays, but safety and protecting the merchandise comes first.

I've recently switched our entire local fleet to specialized bike couriers that use fully waterproof heavy-duty fiberglass boxes. It's more expensive than standard bags, but since the switch, we've had zero damaged electronics during the transit.

Also, a tip for my fellow Lagosians: We've started using a 'Rainy Day Surcharge' which we use to offer bonuses to riders who manage to deliver within reasonable windows without risking safety. Is anyone else seeing success with specialized logistics providers during this peak rainy season?

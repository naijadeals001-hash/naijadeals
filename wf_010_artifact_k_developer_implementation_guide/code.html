Sharding Strategy Notes

We utilize a **Horizontal Key-Based Sharding** approach for the messaging and audit layers. Data is partitioned by `org_id` to ensure localized latency stays under 15ms.

  • Shard 01-10: North America (US-EAST-1)
  • Shard 11-20: European Union (EU-CENTRAL-1)
  • Shard 21-30: APAC (AP-SOUTHEAST-1)

REBALANCE_EVENT: Triggered automatically if any shard exceeds 85% capacity or 1.2M transactions per minute.
